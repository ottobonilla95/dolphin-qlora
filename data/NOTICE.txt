The Bitext data has been modified for this study. Instructions and intent labels
were selected, deduplicated using documented lexical rules, balanced across
intents, shuffled, divided into splits, and formatted as chat messages. Original
long-form responses were replaced by JSON intent-label targets. These changes
are recorded in split-manifest.json, prepare_data.py and build_large_baseline.py.

Files containing modified Bitext data include train.jsonl, valid.jsonl,
../baseline-large-prompts.json, ../evaluation-source/reserved-instructions.json,
and prompts or excerpts retained in ../results/. The original 30-case suite also
contains hand-written examples and general probes created for this experiment.

Data licensing is distinct from model licensing. Model weights and trained
adapter weights are excluded from this Git repository. Obtain model weights
from the pinned upstream source under its applicable terms.
